The honest story

What's proven, and what's tradition.

Clear Quartz · Proven

Nearly pure silicon dioxide, Mohs 7. Piezoelectric like all quartz: the reason quartz crystals keep time in watches and clocks.

Clear Quartz · Traditional

Clarity and focus; often called an "amplifier" stone. Shared as tradition.

Selenite · Proven

A clear form of gypsum (calcium sulfate). Very soft, Mohs 2: it scratches with a fingernail. Shop "selenite" is usually satin spar, the fibrous form.

Selenite · Traditional

Clarity, calm and cleansing a space. Shared as tradition.

Care

Durable. Mild soap and water is fine. Keep it dry. Gypsum slowly dissolves in water. Wipe clean and handle gently.
