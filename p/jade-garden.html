The honest story

What's proven, and what's tradition.

Green Jade · Proven

Jade is two minerals, nephrite or jadeite, Mohs 6 to 7. Famous for toughness: it resists breaking better than most stones.

Green Jade · Traditional

Harmony, balance, good luck and the heart. Shared as tradition.

Purple Jade · Proven

A purple jadeite, colored by traces of manganese. Same tough jade family, Mohs 6.5 to 7.

Purple Jade · Traditional

Calm, intuition and spirit. Shared as tradition.
