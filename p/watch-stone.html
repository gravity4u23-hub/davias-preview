What's proven, and what's tradition.

Yellow Quartz · Proven

Quartz colored golden-yellow by traces of iron (often sold as citrine). Like all quartz it is piezoelectric: pressure makes a small electric charge.

Yellow Quartz · Traditional

Warmth, joy, abundance and clarity. Shared as tradition, not fact.

Care

Durable. Mild soap and water is fine.
