The honest story

What's proven, and what's tradition.

Yellow Quartz · Proven

Quartz colored golden-yellow by traces of iron (often sold as citrine). Like all quartz it is piezoelectric: pressure makes a small electric charge.

Yellow Quartz · Traditional

Warmth, joy, abundance and clarity. Shared as tradition, not fact.

Tiger's Eye · Proven

Quartz, Mohs 7. The moving shimmer (chatoyancy) comes from fine fibers replaced by quartz and iron oxides.

Tiger's Eye · Traditional

Courage, confidence and protection. Shared as tradition.

Green Jade · Proven

Jade is two minerals, nephrite or jadeite, Mohs 6 to 7. Famous for toughness: it resists breaking better than most stones.

Green Jade · Traditional

Harmony, balance, good luck and the heart. Shared as tradition.

Purple Jade · Proven

A purple jadeite, colored by traces of manganese. Same tough jade family, Mohs 6.5 to 7.

Purple Jade · Traditional

Calm, intuition and spirit. Shared as tradition.

Selenite · Proven

A clear form of gypsum (calcium sulfate). Very soft, Mohs 2: it scratches with a fingernail. Shop "selenite" is usually satin spar, the fibrous form.

Selenite · Traditional

Clarity, calm and cleansing a space. Shared as tradition.

Care

Durable. Mild soap and water is fine. Tough stone. Mild soap and water is fine. Keep it dry. Gypsum slowly dissolves in water. Wipe clean and handle gently.
