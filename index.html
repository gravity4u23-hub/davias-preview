The real "energy" fact

Quartz is piezoelectric: squeeze it and it makes a tiny electric charge. That is why quartz keeps time in watches. Tiger's eye, clear and yellow quartz are all quartz.

An honest word on "conductive"

Most gemstones are electrical insulators, not conductors like copper wire. We'd rather tell you the true thing, which is more interesting anyway.

Hardness you can feel

Selenite is Mohs 2, soft enough to scratch with a fingernail. Quartz is Mohs 7, harder than a steel knife. That's why care changes from stone to stone.
