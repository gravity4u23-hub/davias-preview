Stone guide

Every stone, honestly labeled.

Each claim gets one label. Proven means checkable science. Traditional means belief and history, shared as tradition. Then how to care for it, and what we will never claim.

Proven

Real, checkable science: what the mineral is made of, how hard it is, and properties like quartz's piezoelectricity.

Traditional

Centuries of use and belief. We share these because they're part of the stones' story, always labeled as tradition.

Open

What a stone feels like to us, labeled as our experience. Never presented as a fact about you.
